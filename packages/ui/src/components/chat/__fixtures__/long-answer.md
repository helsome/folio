# Quarterly Research Note — Acme Semiconductor (ACME)

## Thesis

We remain constructive on **ACME** as the leading-edge foundry cycle inflects.
The key question is no longer *whether* utilisation recovers, but *how fast* the
mix shifts toward advanced nodes.

> Evidence note: figures below are illustrative and are not investment advice.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Drivers

1. Advanced-node utilisation recovering ahead of consensus.
2. Pricing discipline holding in mature nodes.
3. Capex normalising from a high base.

- Risk: customer concentration in mobile.
- Risk: export-control regime changes.
- Risk: FX translation on a USD-heavy backlog.
